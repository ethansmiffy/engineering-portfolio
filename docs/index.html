About my work

My work combines mechanical modelling with computational methods. After leading a five-person structural health monitoring project, I independently extended the research over summer to compare an LSTM model against a published NARX approach.

Alongside my degree, I’m reconstructing a model Shay steam engine in Autodesk Fusion 360 from inherited engineering drawings. The ongoing project covers 368 unique components and 23 functional subassemblies.
